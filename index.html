Open-source tools for seeing what AI work costs before you run it, and for doing the same work with fewer tokens. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

Token Estimator

Understand the cost before you run.

One product, three modules on one shared pricing and counting engine: Estimator (forecast a task's tokens and cost), Workflows (forecast a multi-step agent run), and Minimiser (shrink the prompt, cap the reply, re-estimate). The loop: Estimate, Minimise, Re-estimate, Run. The dashboard is the full product: the estimator, Workflows and the Minimiser in one place. The quick estimate prices a single prompt on its own.
